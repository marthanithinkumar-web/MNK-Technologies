## Official products

- **Public Online Service Provider (POSP)** — Live
- **FileWeave** — Live

The official company identity and portfolio are defined in [`company.json`](./company.json). Company positioning, branding, product-status rules and release standards are defined in [`COMPANY_FOUNDATION.md`](./COMPANY_FOUNDATION.md).

Only products explicitly listed in `company.json` are part of the official MNK Technologies portfolio. Unrelated repositories, experiments, academic projects and prototypes are not automatically company products.

Automated CI verifies the public hostname, sitemap, responsive layout, legal wording, Google Search Console verification tag, and consistency between the portfolio source of truth and the public website.

MNK Technologies is not represented as a private limited or incorporated company.
